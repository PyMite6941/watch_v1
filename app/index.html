Welcome

Not connected

USB: plug a data cable into the USB-C socket on the watch's right-hand side and press a watch button. Bluetooth: hold both watch buttons for 2 seconds until it shows LInk. Demo: a pretend watch inside this page, for trying things out.

This browser cannot talk to USB or Bluetooth devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer; the demo watch still works here.

Quick setup

Four things and the watch is ready. Everything here can be changed later under Settings.

This watch

Wi-Fi networks

Up to 5. The watch joins the strongest one it knows, only to fetch the time. Passwords stay on the watch.

Screens

Click to turn a screen on or off. MODE on the watch steps through them in this order, then through your apps. Turn one off and on again to move it to the end. Screens marked * need a pack.

Personal

Bell schedule

The watch vibrates at these times, and the "Next bell" screen counts down to the next one.

Weather

Add the Weather screen above, give a place, and the watch fetches the weather over Wi-Fi every hour or so. On the watch: SET steps through now, sky, high, low, rain chance, sunrise, sunset and air quality; holding SET fetches it now. The place you give is sent to the weather service.

Watch-to-watch (mesh)

Kits

Ready-made set-ups: screens, settings and apps chosen for one kind of person. Loading a kit changes only what it lists and adds its apps; the watch's name, time zone, Wi-Fi and PIN stay as they are. What to build and print for each: kits on GitHub.

Your apps

Each app is an extra screen on the watch, after the built-in ones. No programming: pick a kind, fill it in, save. Up to 8.

Steps

On the watch: SET starts, SET again skips to the next step, hold SET to stop. It buzzes and shows the label at every change, even if the watch was asleep.

Shows the days left, for example d 42; on the last day, hours and minutes.

Letters the display can show

Four seven-segment digits cannot draw every letter. These work; anything else shows as a blank.

Files on the watch

Text files only (JSON, CSV, notes), and small ones: the watch has about 190 kB. Files in /apps become screens; anything else is simply kept for you, or for a pack's own page.

Live readings

The watch sends its sensors to this page as they change. Record a run and open it in a spreadsheet.

Click a reading to graph it. The graph shows the last 10 minutes.

Accessory socket

Lists every I²C device that answers on the socket — the first thing to check when a new pack does nothing.

Backup and copy

One file holds this watch's settings and every file on it. Keep it as a backup, or restore it onto another watch to make a twin. Wi-Fi passwords and the PIN are never included.

Start over
