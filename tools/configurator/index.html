USB: plug a data cable into the watch's USB-C port (the one on the right-hand side, not the charging port) and press a watch button to wake it. Bluetooth: hold both watch buttons for 2 seconds until it shows LInk.

2. Wi-Fi networks

Up to 5. The watch joins whichever saved network is strongest, only to fetch the time. Passwords are stored on the watch and never sent back.

3. Screens

Click to turn a screen on or off; MODE on the watch steps through them in the order shown. Turn one off and on again to move it to the end.
